📄 Activity 7: Resume Generating

𖠿 Overview

For this activity, I explored how Large Language Models (LLMs) can help create professional resumes using personal information, educational background, skills, and clinical experiences.

I used ChatGPT and Claude AI to generate a one-page professional resume targeting an Operating Room (OR) Staff Nurse position. After generating the resumes, I reviewed and compared their outputs to determine which one best presented my qualifications.

✧ Objective

The objective of this activity was to explore how different AI tools can assist in creating professional documents and understand the importance of reviewing AI-generated content.

🤖 AI Tools Used

* ChatGPT – Used to generate a professional resume based on the provided personal and nursing-related information.
* Claude AI – Used to generate another resume using the same information for comparison.

✧ Resume Content

The information provided to the AI tools included:

* Professional summary
* Educational background
* Clinical nursing experience
* Areas of clinical exposure
* Technical skills and competencies
* Training and certifications

The resume was prepared for an OR Staff Nurse position, highlighting relevant clinical experiences and nursing skills.

✧ Comparison and Final Output

Both AI tools generated organized resumes using the same information. However, I selected the ChatGPT-generated resume as my final output because I preferred its more formal presentation, organized sections, and emphasis on clinical experiences relevant to the target position.

The final resume presents my educational background, clinical nursing experience, areas of exposure, technical skills, and training in a professional format.

📄 What I Learned

Through this activity, I learned that different LLMs can produce different outputs even when given the same information. AI tools can help organize and improve professional documents, but their outputs still need to be reviewed to ensure that the information is accurate and reflects my actual qualifications.

AI can help present your qualifications, but reviewing the output makes sure it truly represents you.
