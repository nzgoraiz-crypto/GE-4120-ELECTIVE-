𖠿 About the Activity

This activity focuses on exploring how Artificial Intelligence (AI) responds to different prompts and how improving the way we ask questions can affect the results.

I started by giving the AI a simple and general prompt. After observing its response, I tried creating a more detailed and specific prompt using an LLM (Large Language Model) to improve the instructions and get a more relevant and organized output.

✧ What I Did

01. Initial Prompt

I first provided the AI with a simple prompt containing only basic instructions. The response gave me a general idea of what I was asking for, but it lacked some of the details I wanted.

02. Improved Prompt

For the second attempt, I made the prompt more detailed by providing clearer instructions, additional context, and specific expectations. I used an LLM to help improve the structure and clarity of my prompt.

03. Comparing the Results

I compared the responses from both attempts and observed how the improved prompt helped the AI understand the task better and generate a more specific and organized response.

☕ What I Learned

Through this activity, I learned that the quality of an AI response is influenced by how a prompt is written. Simple prompts can provide general answers, while detailed prompts with clear instructions and context can produce more relevant and useful results.

I also learned that LLMs can help improve prompts, making communication with AI more effective.
