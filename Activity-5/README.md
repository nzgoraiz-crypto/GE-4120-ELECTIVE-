🤖 Activity 4: Exploring and Improving AI Prompts

𖠿 Overview

For this activity, I explored how prompts influence the responses generated by artificial intelligence. I used a Large Language Model (LLM) to compare the results of a simple prompt with a more detailed and specific one.

The goal was to understand how the way we ask questions or give instructions can affect the quality and relevance of AI-generated responses.

✧ Prompt Exploration

I started by entering a simple prompt into the AI tool and observing the response it generated.

After that, I improved the prompt by adding more details, instructions, and context. This allowed me to compare how the AI responded to different levels of information.

💡 What I Did

1. Created an Initial Prompt – Entered a simple instruction into the AI tool.
2. Reviewed the Response – Observed the output and identified areas that could be improved.
3. Improved the Prompt – Added clearer instructions, context, and specific details.
4. Compared the Results – Examined the differences between the initial and improved responses.
5. Evaluated the Output – Considered which response was more relevant and useful.

✧ Key Takeaways

Through the activity, I learned that:

* Clear instructions help guide AI-generated responses.
* Providing context can make the output more relevant.
* Specific prompts can produce more organized results.
* Reviewing and improving prompts is part of using AI effectively.
* AI responses should still be checked and evaluated before being used.

✧ Purpose

The purpose of this activity was to demonstrate the importance of effective prompting when using AI tools. It showed how improving the instructions given to an AI can lead to responses that better match the intended task.

🤖 What I Learned

This activity helped me understand that using AI is not just about entering a question and accepting the first response. It also involves knowing how to communicate instructions clearly, provide enough context, and evaluate the generated output.

I learned that a better prompt can make a difference in the response we receive.

Better prompts lead to more useful responses.
